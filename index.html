  • Compare available photos and options.

An Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet becomes more practical when the item price sits beside applicable delivery charges and other estimated costs.

  • Keep confirmed prices separate from estimates.
  • Check shipping, fees and destination charges.
  • Review the shortlist before committing.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, an Oopbuy spreadsheet means a spreadsheet-style shortlist for organizing product links, categories, measurements, photos and estimated costs. This page provides category links and planning advice rather than a downloadable spreadsheet.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS categories. Other navigation and shopping links open the CAFANS homepage.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Use the measurement chart supplied for the specific listing. Compare clothing measurements with an item you own, and compare footwear measurements using the listing’s stated method. Letter sizes and size numbers alone may be insufficient.

Does a product photo verify authenticity or quality?

No. Photos can help you inspect visible details, but they do not independently establish authenticity, material composition, durability or seller reliability.

What should I include in my shopping budget?

Record the item price and check applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion and destination taxes or duties. Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS discovery guide. It is not an official Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website.
